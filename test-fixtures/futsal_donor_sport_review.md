# The Effect of Donor Sports on Talent Development: A Systematic Review

## Abstract

Background: Donor sports—sports that transfer skills to a target sport—have been increasingly studied in talent development literature. Futsal, as a constrained version of football, is frequently cited as a potential donor sport. However, the evidence base remains fragmented.

Objective: This systematic review synthesizes empirical evidence on skill transfer from donor sports to target sports, with a focus on futsal-to-football transfer.

Methods: Following PRISMA guidelines, we searched PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science for studies published between 2000-2025. Inclusion criteria: empirical studies examining skill transfer between sports, published in English. Exclusion criteria: opinion pieces, non-peer-reviewed sources, studies not measuring transfer outcomes. A total of 847 records were screened, with 23 studies meeting inclusion criteria.

Results: Three key themes emerged from the synthesis: (1) Perceptual-cognitive transfer—futsal players demonstrated superior decision-making speed and visual search efficiency in football contexts (8 studies, effect sizes d=0.42-0.78); (2) Technical transfer—ball control, passing accuracy, and first-touch quality showed significant positive transfer (11 studies, d=0.35-0.65); (3) Tactical transfer—spatial awareness and off-ball movement patterns transferred moderately (4 studies, d=0.28-0.51).

Discussion: The findings support the donor sport hypothesis, particularly for perceptual-cognitive skills. The constrained nature of futsal (smaller pitch, heavier ball, fewer players) appears to accelerate the development of decision-making capacities that later benefit football performance. However, the evidence for tactical transfer is weaker, suggesting that some sport-specific tactical knowledge may not transfer automatically.

Conclusion: Futsal shows consistent evidence of positive skill transfer to football, particularly in perceptual-cognitive and technical domains. Practitioners should consider integrating futsal into football talent development pathways, with attention to the age at which transfer effects are maximized.
